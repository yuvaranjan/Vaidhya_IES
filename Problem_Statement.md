**Vaidhya (CureCloud): Offline-First AI Telemedicine & Rural Clinical Decision Support Platform** `Hard`

Executive Background India's rural healthcare system faces a structural crisis: the majority of the population lives in areas served by a fraction of the country's registered doctors, and village health centers are typically staffed by nurses or community health workers with no on-site physician and no reliable internet connectivity for large parts of the day. Existing telemedicine platforms assume continuous broadband and smartphone-literate patients; both assumptions fail at the last mile, where the need is greatest. The result is delayed diagnosis, unnecessary travel to distant cities for routine consultations, and preventable deterioration of treatable conditions. Industry Context The platform should simulate deployment within: Rural Primary Health Centers Village Sub-Centers Telemedicine Networks connecting rural centers to city-based specialists Community Health Worker-assisted care settings The solution must prioritize genuine offline functionality at the point of care while preserving patient data integrity and clinical trust. Challenge Statement Design and develop a production-minded, offline-capable AI teleconsultation system that lets a nurse-assisted patient at a rural health center complete a full diagnostic intake with an AI voicebot, reach a city-based doctor through the best available connectivity mode — including a pure low-bandwidth text mode as a first-class path, not a fallback — and receive a prescription that routes into a real local pharmacy fulfillment loop. The system should assist doctors with specialty-scoped AI second opinions while keeping the doctor in full clinical control, and must degrade gracefully as connectivity degrades rather than failing outright.

**Requirements**

Functional Requirements The platform should support: ●Voice-based conversational patient intake ●NLP-driven symptom extraction ●Nurse-mediated vitals and exam finding capture ●Vitals-driven adaptive questioning ●Visual symptom capture via still image ●Automated diagnostic summary generation ●Urgency/severity signaling ●Doctor waiting-room queue with doctor-initiated pickup ●Cascading connection modes (video/audio/low-bandwidth text) ●In-consult follow-up relay through the AI voicebot ●Prescription issuance and pharmacy routing ●Pharmacist stock, queue, and billing management ●Patient medical history timeline

Technical Expectations Teams should engineer a system capable of: Offline-First Data Architecture ●Two-tier edge/central data topology ●Local-first intake with zero live-connectivity dependency ●Defined reconciliation for out-of-jurisdiction/unsynced records. Low-Bandwidth Consultation Protocol ●Pub/sub messaging (MQTT) as a primary channel ●Message delivery guarantees ●Reconnect/resume mid-consultation ●Bounded timeout-to-requeue fallback. Clinical Reasoning Support ●Specialty-scoped AI advisory opinions on demand ●Grounded in the full diagnostic report ●Explicit non-diagnostic, doctor-in-control framing. Explainable AI Every AI-generated clinical output must include: ●Confidence indicator ●Plain-language evidence trace ●Decision trace. Concurrency & Fault Tolerance ●Non-destructive resolution of simultaneous doctor pickups ●Bounded timeout-and-proceed on nurse non-response or connection drops. Regional Health Signal Aggregation ●Multi-region anonymized case data dashboard ●Statistically-defined anomaly detection against rolling baseline. Patient Outreach ●Real SMS/IVR delivery for prescriptions and follow-ups ●Event-triggered, decoupled from point-of-care connectivity state

Non-Functional Requirements The solution should demonstrate: ●Genuine offline functionality, not just connectivity tolerance ●Graceful degradation across connectivity tiers ●Data integrity under concurrent multi-user access ●Auditable, non-black-box AI outputs ●Clear separation of demo-scope simplifications from target production architecture

Expected Deliverables ●Functional multi-portal platform (Patient, Doctor, Pharmacist) ●Working low-bandwidth text consultation demonstrated end-to-end with simulated disconnect/reconnect ●AI voicebot intake with adaptive questioning ●Specialist advisory module with explainability output ●Regional health analytics dashboard with seeded anomaly ●Live SMS/IVR triggered on a real device ●Architecture documentation

Evaluation Metrics Solutions will be assessed based on: ●Real-world applicability to rural connectivity/staffing constraints ●Robustness of offline/degraded-connectivity behavior, demonstrated live ●Quality and honesty of AI explainability ●System design maturity ●Clinical workflow realism ●User experience for low-digital-literacy patients and clinical staff ●Production readiness of the architecture
